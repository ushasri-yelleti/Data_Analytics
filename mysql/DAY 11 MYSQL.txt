DATE & TIME FUNCTIONS:--
--> CURDATE()--> current date
--> CURTIME()--> current time
--> NOW()--> current date nd time
--> DATE()--> extracted date part
--> time()--> extracted time part
--> YEAR()--> extract year
--> month()--> extract month
--> day()/dayofmonth()--> extract day of the month
--> week()--> extract week no.
--> dayofweek()--> extract day of the week
--> date_add()--> add time to a date
--> date_sub()--> subtract time frm a date
--> datediff()--> difference btw 2 dates
--> timestampdiff()--> diff btw 2 datetime values
