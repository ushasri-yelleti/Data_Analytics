DATA:
data  is raw facts or figures without context.
eg: 101, samitha, 50000, vsp

RECORD:

eg: (101,'samitha',50000)

FIELD:
field or attribute is called as columns, smallest unit of data in database.
eg: empname, empaddress, empsal.

DATABASE:
database is a collection of of data(company info)
