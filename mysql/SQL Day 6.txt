My SQL DCL COMMANDS

DATA CONTROL LANGUAGE --  DCL
are used to control access to data in the database-mainly to grant or revoke privileges to users.
GRANT - gives permission
REVOKE - removes previously granted privileges

COMMON PRIVILEGES:
SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE AND ALL PRIVILEGES - FULL ACCESS.

USER CREATION:
CREATE USER "USERNAME" IDENTIFIED BY "PASSWORD"

GRANT
GRANT PRIVILEGE ON TABLE_NAME TO "USERNAME"
EG: GRANT SELECT,INSERT,UPDATE ON STUDENTS TO RAMU;

REVOKE
REVOKE PRIVILEGE ON TABLE_NAME FROM "USERNAME"
